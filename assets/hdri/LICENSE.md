# Built-in HDRIs

All environment maps in this folder are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/hdris) and are
released under **CC0** (public domain). They are stored here downscaled to 1K (1024×512,
Radiance `.hdr`); the originals are available at higher resolutions on Poly Haven.
